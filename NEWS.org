#+title: redline news

* 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First release.

- =C-c * c= comments on a region that crosses paragraphs or other changes, as a run of highlights that exports as one Word comment over the whole passage.  On a comment, it adds a reply instead of refusing.
- Comments in the same paragraph no longer show as an org-modern target (grey box and ↪).

- Import a .docx as org with CriticMarkup, including comment threads, resolved comments, and inline objects.
- Export back to a copy of the original as real Word tracked changes, keeping the author's formatting.
- =redline-mode=: tracking on in files from Word, review pane with accept, reject, resolve, and delete, margin author tags, clean view, replies.
